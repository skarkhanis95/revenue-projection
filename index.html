Forecast assumes users remain subscribed after joining, no churn, no taxes, and no additional variable costs. YoY percentages compare a month with the same month 12 months earlier; they are shown as “—” until a prior-year comparison exists or when the prior-year value is zero.
How the model works: Total paying users in month N = new users per month × N. Monthly revenue = total paying users × subscription price. Monthly profit = revenue − fixed monthly expenses. Cumulative totals sum each month's values. YoY growth compares each month against the corresponding month one year earlier, rather than comparing against the immediately preceding month.
